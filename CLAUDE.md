# Working rules for this repo

Rules promoted out of `.learnings/MISTAKES.md` once they have earned a place
that binds every session rather than a record of one. Short on purpose: a rule
nobody re-reads is not a rule.

## Run a named process as written

When I name a skill, a command, or a process — `/impeccable`, `/ponytail`, a
slash command, "do it the way X does" — run it as written. Every gate, every
pause, every confirmation step.

Do not compress, reorder, or drop a step because:

- the answer looks obvious
- the brief seems complete
- pausing feels like friction
- you are confident the direction is right

Confidence that the direction is right is not authority to skip the step where
I get to say otherwise. A gate that turns out to be unnecessary costs one
message; a gate that was necessary costs a rebuild.

If a step genuinely cannot be run in this environment, say so **before**
starting and ask what to do instead. Never announce a skip after the work is
already done.
